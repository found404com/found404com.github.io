Support

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Help with recording, Pro purchases, and local participant data. No Drop In account is required.

Contact support

Email brazier_jests8l@icloud.com. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, steps to reproduce, and any error message. For Watch issues, include the Watch model and watchOS version. Do not send passwords, payment card details, face templates, or sensitive recordings.

Camera, microphone, or Photos access

In iPhone Settings, find Drop In and check its permissions. Camera and microphone permissions are also listed under Privacy & Security. Reopen the app after a change. If audio is missing, check both microphone permission and the Pro mute setting. Photos permission is needed for saving; matching albums may require broader Photos access than adding a video alone.

Saving or sharing a video

Select a video in the gallery and use save to Photos or share. Manual export is free; automatic saving is Pro. Check permissions and free device storage if saving fails. Verify that the exported video plays before deleting the original. Shared filenames and Photos album names may include a participant’s name.

Pro is missing or purchases will not load

Check your internet connection and the Apple Account used for purchases. In Drop In Settings, choose Restore Purchases under Drop In Pro, or use the same button on the Pro screen. A pending purchase may be waiting for approval or payment completion. Do not buy again to troubleshoot. See Apple’s restore help. Restoration unlocks eligible Pro access, not local recordings.

Cancel a subscription or request a refund

Use Manage Subscription in Drop In, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Drop In. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Removing the app does not cancel billing. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Apple handles cancellations and refund requests.

Apple Watch Remote

Watch control requires Pro. Keep the iPhone app open and active, make sure the Watch is paired and connected, and check camera access, battery, and storage. Restore Pro on the iPhone if the Watch remains locked.

Group Training or model download

Group Training requires Pro and the optional AuraFace model. Use a reliable connection, preferably Wi-Fi, and enough free storage; confirm the model download in the app. The model screen can fetch its manifest before confirmation. If download is not configured or unavailable, contact support with the exact message; ordinary recording does not require this model.

For recognition, use good lighting and show one unobstructed face. Retry or register the participant again if needed; review the selected participant before recording or sharing. Recognition is not identity verification.

Delete local data

Open Settings → Privacy → Participant data. You can use the deletion controls without Pro and without an installed face model.

Delete videos from the gallery. In Manage participants, delete one profile or all participants and embeddings; existing videos remain, without the deleted participant assignment. Use Delete downloaded face model and Clear Core ML cache for those separate files. Disabling Group Training or ending Pro does not erase them.

Export important videos before deleting the app. iOS Delete App removes local app data; Offload App keeps it. Remove Photos copies, shared files, and backups separately where applicable. There is no Drop In account to close, and support cannot remotely erase your device. See the Privacy Policy.
