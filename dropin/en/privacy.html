Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Overview

Drop In is an iOS and Apple Watch app for recording video on your iPhone and controlling recording from Apple Watch. The app is designed to work locally on your devices and does not require an account.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Drop In may access, how it is used, and what choices you have.

2. Camera, microphone, and Photos permissions

Drop In requests camera access for preview, video recording, and optional participant recognition, and microphone access to record audio. You can change permissions in iOS Settings; the in-app microphone mute control is a Pro feature.

Photos access is requested when you save a video or enable Pro auto-save. Saving without an album uses add-only access. Saving into a matching album may require read/write access to find or create that album; its name may contain an album or participant name.

3. Videos and local gallery

Videos recorded with Drop In are saved locally inside the app unless you choose to save or share them elsewhere. The local gallery may store videos, thumbnails, durations, save status, and album assignments.

Video and audio you record may include personal information about you or other people, such as faces, voices, locations, surroundings, or activities. You are responsible for what you choose to record, save, or share.

4. Albums and settings

Drop In may store app settings on your device, including recording delay, duration limit, camera quality, focus mode, microphone setting, energy saving mode, keep-screen-awake setting, volume-button start setting, auto-save setting, album names, emoji badges, visibility, order, and selected album.

5. Apple Watch remote control

The Drop In Watch app acts as a remote control for the iPhone app. To provide this feature, Drop In may sync recording state, connection state, countdown, elapsed time, remaining time, selected album, visible album list, haptics setting, and remote-control commands between your iPhone and Apple Watch.

6. Group Training and face templates

Optional Group Training, a Pro feature, uses Apple Vision and AuraFace on the iPhone to associate newly recorded videos with a participant and show personal galleries. Local participant profiles contain a name, optional emoji and color, identifiers, dates, active status, capture settings, model version, and numerical face templates (embeddings).

Face scans and crops are processed in memory, not saved as participant photos. Your recorded videos and their thumbnails may still show faces. Participant names and templates are not sent to a recognition service. Recognition can be wrong: it is gallery organization, not biometric authentication, identity verification, or spoofing protection. Obtain participants’ permission before registering their faces.

7. Optional model and network requests

The AuraFace model file is downloaded over HTTPS only after explicit download confirmation. Opening or refreshing the model screen can request a manifest describing the model before that confirmation. These requests do not contain your videos, face images, templates, or participant names.

The server delivering the manifest or model receives technical request information, such as an IP address, time, requested file, and HTTP headers, and may keep access logs. Downloaded model files and runtime caches are stored on the device. Recognition runs locally after installation; downloading, updating, or checking model availability can need internet access.

The manifest and model are delivered through GitHub Releases and GitHub’s download infrastructure. Technical requests are subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Apple-managed purchases

Apple handles Pro payments through the App Store. Drop In uses StoreKit product and transaction information, such as product identifiers, purchase validity, subscription expiration or revocation, and a locally cached Pro status, to unlock or restore features. The app does not receive your payment card details. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy practices.

9. Support and legal website

These support and legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening an Apple help link takes you to Apple’s website, with its own privacy practices.

10. How information is used

Information is used to provide camera preview, recording, countdown, automatic stop, local gallery, album filtering, video playback, sharing, saving to Photos, Watch remote control, haptics, sounds, error alerts, and app settings you choose to use.

11. Local processing

Drop In does not require an app account or provide its own cloud gallery. Video, audio, album data, settings, participant profiles, and face templates are processed on your device, not uploaded to the developer for processing. This does not mean the app makes no network requests: model delivery and Apple purchases are described above, and exports, system backups, and support emails are described below.

12. Sharing and export

Sharing or saving to Photos sends the video to your chosen destination. Shared filenames and matching Photos album names can include participant or album names. Photos, iCloud Photos, device backups, and other destinations may store or sync data according to your settings and their own privacy practices. Drop In does not control those copies.

13. Data sharing and sale

Drop In does not sell your recordings, albums, settings, participant profiles, or face templates, or share them for advertising or profiling. Recognition templates are used to organize your local gallery, not for advertising.

14. Analytics and diagnostics

The app does not integrate advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Apple or the operating system may provide diagnostics and crash information according to your system privacy settings; this is separate from your recorded videos.

15. Data retention

Open Settings → Privacy → Participant data for the deletion controls. This route remains available without Pro and without an installed face model.

Local recordings, settings, participant profiles, and templates remain until you remove them or delete the app. In Manage participants, you can delete a profile or all participants and templates. Deleting a participant leaves existing videos unassigned; it does not delete those videos. The downloaded model and Core ML cache have separate deletion controls. Turning off Group Training or cancelling Pro does not itself erase these files.

Delete videos in the gallery and remove exported copies separately in Photos or other destinations. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local data; backups and exported copies may remain. See deletion help.

16. Children

Drop In is not intended for use by children under the age required by local law to use recording apps, manage App Store downloads, or consent to recording without parental consent.

17. Security

Drop In uses platform-provided protections from iOS, watchOS, and Photos. However, no device or software system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect your device, Photos library, and shared videos appropriately.

18. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

19. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, email brazier_jests8l@icloud.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments to handle your request. Avoid sending face templates or sensitive recordings. We cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your device.
